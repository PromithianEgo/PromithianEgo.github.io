Project 01 / MOBILITY

A mobility system built around intent.

Role Creator / Product / Design / Engineering
Products 2 Applications
Platform Native iOS
Focus Mobility / Scheduling

The Product Idea

Mobility applications can become visually and cognitively dense very quickly. Vehicle classes, live maps, pricing states, scheduling options, account controls, route information, and promotional content all compete for attention. The opportunity was to build the opposite: a ride experience that reveals only what the customer needs to make the next decision.

Application 01

The Operative

Consumer-facing booking experience for immediate and scheduled transportation.

01

Home / Booking

The home view keeps the booking experience focused on the essential trip inputs. Pickup, destination, and ride timing are brought together in one controlled surface so the customer can define the journey without moving through unnecessary screens.

Product Decision

Concentrate the primary booking decisions into one surface and reveal additional complexity only when the customer's intent requires it.

02

Checkout

Checkout appears only after the system has resolved the meaningful ride variables. The customer can review the trip, fare, and scheduling state in one place before making the final commitment.

Product Decision

Resolve route, availability, timing, and fare before asking the customer to confirm the ride.

Application 02

The Operative Console

Internal interface supporting ride intake, schedule visibility, and operational action.

03

Operational View

The internal application does not mirror the customer experience. It reorganizes the same underlying system around the operator's next decision.

Product Decision

One product system can—and should—present different realities to different users.

Decision Record

What shaped the product.

Problem

Traditional ride experiences surface too many controls before the customer has established basic intent.

Chosen Direction

Progressive disclosure with separate immediate and scheduled booking paths.

Deliberately Avoided

Vehicle-class shopping, promotional clutter, persistent navigation, and premature account controls.

Product Principle

The customer should never solve a problem the product can solve first.

The Product Idea

Most digital commerce assumes that more products, more filters, and more visible inventory create a better buying experience. Rehparto explores the opposite: fewer choices, stronger presentation, and a transaction path that protects the customer's attention.

Application 03

Rehparto

A cinematic purchase experience centered on one intentional product at a time.

01

Home Screen

The home screen establishes the storefront as a curated product experience rather than a dense catalog. The interface keeps attention on the active products and gives the customer a direct path into exploration without introducing unnecessary navigation or visual noise.

Product Decision

Keep discovery focused by presenting the product experience as a controlled selection rather than an inventory-heavy storefront.

02

Product Detail

The product detail screen gives the selected item its own focused environment. Product information is available when the customer asks for it, while the broader storefront remains visually restrained and the path back to discovery stays intact.

Product Decision

Separate product evaluation from product discovery so additional information appears only after the customer has expressed interest in a specific item.

03

Checkout

Checkout preserves the customer's selected products and moves directly toward purchase confirmation. The transaction remains part of the same product experience rather than becoming a detached flow, protecting continuity from selection through completion.

Product Decision

Preserve selection state through checkout so the customer can complete the transaction without rebuilding context or repeating earlier decisions.

Application 04

Rehparto Console

Internal application supporting intake, fulfillment visibility, and transaction management.

04

Fulfillment

The internal application strips away the storefront's theatrical language and presents only what is necessary to fulfill the customer's promise correctly.

Product Decision

Brand expression and operational efficiency can belong to the same system without using the same interface.

Decision Record

What shaped the product.

Problem

Customers can feel more confident when a commerce experience narrows attention instead of maximizing visible choice.

Chosen Direction

One-product-at-rest discovery with persistent state and a deliberately narrow product assortment.

Avoided

Dense product grids, infinite feeds, filters, promotions, and traditional e-commerce chrome.

Product Principle

Curation is a product decision, not merely a visual style.

Bring the working day into one surface.

Role Creator / Product / Design / Engineering
Products 1 Application

The Product Idea

Daily work is often distributed across calendars, task lists, notes, and connected services. Dashboard brings those high-value surfaces together without flattening them into one overloaded screen. The product uses a small number of deliberate containers so the user can understand the day, capture context, and move into connected tools without losing focus.

Application 05

Ahtemporal Cockpit

A focused iOS workspace connecting calendar, tasks, notes, and integrations through a restrained container-based interface.

01

Daily Overview

The opening view establishes the application identity first, then immediately moves into the information that shapes the day. Calendar context and the active task list share the primary surface so planning and execution remain visually connected.

Product Decision

Put the day-defining information first and keep the primary home surface limited to the containers that most directly influence what happens next.

02

Notes + Integrations

The secondary home content extends the workspace without changing its visual language. Notes provide an immediate place for working context, while the integrations container creates a controlled bridge into the external services connected to the app.

Product Decision

Keep reference material and connected services available from the same home environment without allowing either to compete with the calendar and task hierarchy above them.

03

Integrations

The integrations view gives connected services their own dedicated surface once the user chooses to manage them. The home screen remains restrained, while the deeper view can clearly show the services available to extend the application.

Product Decision

Keep integration management out of the primary dashboard until the user explicitly enters that task, preserving a quieter everyday experience.

Decision Record

What shaped the product.

Problem

Calendar context, active tasks, working notes, and connected services often live in separate places, forcing the user to repeatedly switch context during the day.

Chosen Direction

A modular home experience built from a small number of high-value containers, with deeper integration management separated into its own view.

Deliberately Avoided

Dense widget walls, deep navigation, duplicated information, and exposing every connected service directly on the primary dashboard.

Product Principle

A dashboard should reduce context switching, not recreate every tool inside one screen.
